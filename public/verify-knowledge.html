Owner check: your knowledge base (document search)

Runs the knowledge base in production, with your own signed-in session. It stores a few small, clearly named test documents (NORIA CHECK …) in your account, searches them by exact words and by meaning, sends the search through Noria's own tool path (planner rules, permission gates, injection defence), checks that a document containing hostile instructions cannot steer anything, and checks the citation verifier. Afterwards it deletes exactly the documents it made, and confirms your own documents are untouched.

Whether the "meaning" search really uses Cloudflare's embedding model is measured and reported, not assumed: if embeddings are unavailable the check says so and only keyword search is verified. Session tokens are never shown or copied.
