How this stays honest. Place records come from the GeoNames gazetteer (via Open-Meteo geocoding); the current temperature is Noria’s reality layer cross-checking Open-Meteo and MET Norway at the exact coordinates and returning a value only when they agree. Population and elevation are shown only when the record has them. A name with no match returns nothing — never a guessed place. Local times are computed live from the place’s time zone.
